# Welcome to Bravo Healthcare

Bravo Healthcare represents Bravo Home Health and Bravo Residential. We provide compassionate care and dependable support at home and in residential settings, helping individuals live safely, comfortably, and independently.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to improve the quality of life of every individual we serve by providing personalized care that promotes comfort, dignity, and well-being for clients and their caregivers.

## BRAVO HOME HEALTH

### Professional Care in the Comfort of Home

Bravo Home Health provides compassionate, home-based healthcare and services to help individuals safely manage their health, maintain independence, and remain comfortable at their residence.

### Home Health Services

Services may include the following based on the individual’s assessed needs, physician’s orders, eligibility, authorization, and the agency’s approved service categories.

### Skilled and Private Duty Nursing

We provide skilled and extended-hour nursing care for children and adults with medical and complex care needs. Services may include:

Health assessments and vital-sign monitoring

Medication administration and education

Chronic disease, wound, and post-surgical care

Tracheostomy, ventilator, oxygen, and respiratory care

G-tube feeding, catheter, and ostomy care

Seizure, aspiration, infection, and skin precautions

Patient and caregiver education

Coordination with physicians and the healthcare team

### Personal Assistance Services

Our care professionals support daily living and independence through:

Bathing, grooming, dressing, and toileting

Mobility, transfers, and walking assistance

Meal preparation and feeding support

Medication reminders

Light housekeeping and laundry

Appointment and activity assistance

Respite care and companionship

### Therapy Services

When authorized and available, therapy services may include:

Physical therapy

Occupational therapy

Speech-language pathology

Therapy services are designed to help individuals improve or maintain mobility, communication, swallowing, strength, coordination, safety, and daily functioning.

### Medical Social Services

Medical social services may assist individuals and families with:

Psychosocial assessments

Community-resource identification

Long-term care planning

Financial and social-resource referrals

Adjustment to illness or disability

Coordination with community organizations

Patient and family support
